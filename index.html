Booth

Two decks and a crossfader for YouTube and YouTube Music tracks.

You opened this as a local file. YouTube refuses to play embeds from file://. In this folder run python3 -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000/booth.html.

MIDI DJ controller

Not connected. Works in Chrome and Edge, and on Android over USB.

Press Learn beside a control, then move or press the matching knob, fader, pad or button on your controller. Mappings are saved in this browser. Faders may jump on first touch.

Search YouTube Music (optional)

Search needs your own YouTube Data API key. It stays in this browser only.

Keys: Q play A, W cue A, P play B, O cue B, ← → crossfader, F auto fade. Click an empty hot cue to set it, click again to jump, Shift-click to clear.
